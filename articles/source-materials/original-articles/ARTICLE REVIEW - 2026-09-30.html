The corrections that matter most

  • Mindcuber: the 12 actions use the quarter-turn metric, whose bound is 26 rather than 20. Random scramble length is an upper bound, not the shortest solution distance. Reverse-scramble supervised labels alone are not Autodidactic Iteration. The DeepCube work was at UC Irvine, not Caltech. The policy table’s ranks also needed correction.
  • Abliteration: Arditi et al. is a 2024 paper. A 50-token prompt has 50 vectors of 12,288 values; 88 × 2 is 176. The projection example and cosine explanation needed correction. KL divergence is unbounded, and neither a small KL on one dataset nor reduced refusal establishes unchanged capability or real-world dangerousness.
  • Quantization: GB and GiB had been mixed. With exact scales, the 8-bit example’s error is about 0.003937, and the 4-bit example’s error is about 0.071429. NVFP4 differs from INT4 and has scaling overhead. Checkpoint export proves size reduction, not successful inference, speed or preserved quality.
  • Haskell: pure functions are deterministic for the same inputs; external I/O is not guaranteed to be. Haskell supports iteration and variables. A record type does not validate incoming JSON. The custom message format is A2A-inspired rather than protocol-conformant. Code layout and the topic variable were corrected, and incomplete excerpts explicitly labelled.
